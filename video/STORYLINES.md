# Crown demo video: storylines

Spine for all three (from the brief): problem in 15 s → selfie → colours → wigs → wig-off days → shortlist → YouCam APIs + privacy delete. 2:20–2:50, 1920×1080, 30 fps, the real app captured at phone size inside the frame, music and on-screen text only (no voice), free music bed, no paid items.

Persona: "Asha", a YouCam-generated woman with a shaved head (not a real person). All try-ons are real cached YouCam results.

## A. Two weeks before (recommended: a stranger follows one person through one real task)

- Device / form: a moment in someone's day, told as an object-to-object chain (each shot ends on the thing that opens the next).
- The viewer sees Asha's real situation (a date, a price, a "final sale" line), then watches her do it right on her phone: her face, her colours, eight wigs on her, a turban for off days, a shortlist sent to her sister. It feels calm and practical, not sad.
- First 2 s: Asha's portrait fills the frame; a line types over it: "Chemo starts in 14 days."
- Beats: hook (portrait + date) → tension ("Wigs: $200–$3,000. Final sale. No returns.") → reveal (portrait shrinks into the phone: Crown) → proof (upload, colours, tap 3 wigs + slider, shade chips, turban) → payoff (all 8 wigs on her face, one per beat) → shortlist + share → claim ("See it on you before you buy") → how it works (6 YouCam calls + delete) → end card.
- Look: Crown's own dark Interfere surfaces for the app world; Asha's warm grey daylight portrait as the second world; Inter + Newsreader for the one headline.
- Sound: soft, warm instrumental bed, quiet UI ticks on taps, one swell on the 8-wig payoff.

## B. Result first

- Device / form: the result first, then match cuts.
- Opens on the payoff: the same face, eight wigs, cut on the beat with the eyes locked in place. Pull back: it's a phone, it's Crown. Then rewind to why it matters, how it works, and the API flow.
- First 2 s: Asha in the French bob, hard cut to the afro, cut to the waves; the eyes never move.
- Beats: payoff montage → pull back to phone (reveal) → tension (the problem, 3 facts) → proof → wig-off days → shortlist → APIs + privacy → end card.
- Look: the same as A, but the opener is a full-frame face grid on black.
- Sound: a rhythmic bed with a clear beat for the cuts; quieter under the problem section.

## C. One frame that rebuilds (the bold one)

- Device / form: one frame that rebuilds: Asha's portrait never leaves the screen.
- Everything happens around her face: hair changes on her head, the app UI docks beside her as each step happens, labels for each YouCam call appear where they act (colour picks by her brows, wig name over the hair, delete when the photo leaves).
- First 2 s: her bare portrait, centred; a hairline draws in and fills into the French bob.
- Beats: portrait (hook) → problem lines beside her → UI panels dock in per step → 8 wigs swap on her in place → turban → shortlist cards fan out of the frame → API labels collect into the call flow → end card on her portrait.
- Look: one continuous composition; Crown's dark panels against her warm grey photo.
- Sound: one continuous ambient bed, a soft tone on each swap.
